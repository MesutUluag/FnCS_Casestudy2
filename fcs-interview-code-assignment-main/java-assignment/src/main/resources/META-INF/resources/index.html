Create Warehouse ?

Create rules: BUC must be unique · Location must exist · Capacity ≤ location max capacity · Stock ≤ capacity · Location cannot exceed its warehouse limit.

Associate Warehouse → Store → Product ?

Constraint 1: A product can be supplied to one store by at most 2 warehouses.
Constraint 2: A store can be served by at most 3 warehouses (across all products).
Constraint 3: A warehouse can hold at most 5 distinct product types.
Duplicate guard: The exact (warehouse, store, product) triple must be unique.
